A little about us

Local people. High standards. A cleaner you can trust.

Born from a passion for cleaning and the belief that every customer deserves high standards, Squeaky Clean was started in 2020 with a simple vision: to become the best cleaning company in South Yorkshire, not the biggest.

We employ local people to serve the local community. Our team members are DBS checked, inducted and supported with continuous improvement training. We are fully insured and accredited, and we work with every customer to tailor the right cleaning package.
